# MARKET ACADEMY Ω — financial reasoning and market mechanics

## Delivered
24 executable subject lanes: equities, equity valuation, index/ETF construction, futures, options, sovereign bonds, credit, FX, commodity curves, crypto derivatives, order books, execution costs, macro surprises, inflation, portfolio exposure, drawdown, leverage, swaps, yield curves, volatility, corporate fundamentals, causal attribution, backtest integrity and forecasting.

Ten tiers, one million seed addresses per lane/tier, 240 million procedural addresses. This is an address space, not 240 million unique human-authored questions or completed evaluations. Templates can repeat economic concepts and some prompts; higher numeric tiers require signed reconciliation of multiple effects. 24,000 generated questions were structurally tested for reproducibility and valid answer options; no external empirical validity follows.

mcp/market_academy.mjs creates deterministic hypothetical questions and identity-bound replay-verifiable answer receipts. It separates mechanical computations from uncertainty and causal attribution. Students advance a lane after 12 distinct current-tier observations of the same model digest, at least 11 correct. This is narrow template performance, not trading skill. mcp/market_worker.mjs admits exact installed models via physical RAM and Ollama busy checks, bounded four-question schema-constrained batches, and paired comparison against Qwen2.5-Coder 7B. Reference is never silently substituted. Paired results are descriptive and do not establish general 7B parity.

mcp/market_knowledge.mjs supplies 12 original source-reviewed mechanism questions with URL, review date and explanation. Sources include SEC Investor.gov/EDGAR, Federal Reserve, CME, BIS, CFTC, EIA, OIC and ALFRED. Source pages were checked for relevant concepts. No full documents or current market feeds were ingested. Core principles: rates transmit through financial conditions, expectations and positioning matter; no event guarantees a price direction; liquidity and market depth affect fills; futures curve shape is not a guaranteed spot forecast; credit spreads include more than default risk; point-in-time data and independent counterfactual evidence matter.

## Training and evaluation
Each existing Qwen3 0.6B and Granite4 350M family corpus was expanded from 64/16/16 to 96 training / 24 retention / 24 transfer examples. 48 market examples per corpus: 32 training (12 source-reviewed concepts, remaining synthetic and up to eight qualified 7B lessons), eight retention, eight transfer. Existing general questions preserved, exact prompt deduplication and separated generated seed bands. Teacher answers enter training only when independently correct on the separate teacher seed band 600001–800000. Assessment seed band 800001–1000000; no reference assessment answers are reused as lessons. These are answer-only native LoRA examples; rationale metadata is preserved but not yet optimized as long-form reasoning. Repeated templates and changing corpora are not external holdouts.

scripts/market_cycle.mjs is integrated into the existing 30-minute INFINITY scheduler after general swarm curriculum generation and before native training. It rotates one bounded four-question batch among Qwen3 0.6B, Granite4 350M, 7B paired assessment, 7B independent teaching. Audit-only while original acquisition or bootstrap is incomplete, then resource-admitted live batches; no new model downloads, no changes to other loops, no Codex. At current cadence, throughput is finite (at most four questions per half-hour market cycle), not hundreds of millions of model calls. Private receipts and status under market-academy/; receipt cap 16384, archive/rotation and throughput expansion remain future work. The current two family corpora feed native training when available; 4096 logical agent instances are not individually trained by this change.
