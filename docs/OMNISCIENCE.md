# OMNISCIENCE Ω — knowledge expansion and external capability intake

## Knowledge curriculum
72 authored multiple-choice knowledge cards in six divisions (12 each): market, general, survival, versatile practical reasoning, coding, agentic operations. Topics include macro surprises, rate sensitivity, liquidity, derivatives, causal event studies, rollovers and backtest integrity; science, history and sampling; camping essentials, route plans, shelter, navigation, food, weather and cautious decisions; budgeting, coordination, backups and diagnosis; algorithms, SQL parameterization, idempotency, race conditions, dependency integrity and tests; trust boundaries, permission checks, resource limits, teacher provenance and retention. Some cards link public educational sources; general factual cards are authored from established knowledge and not all independently source-reviewed. Survival cards are conservative preparedness education, not professional emergency guidance. NPS reference: https://www.nps.gov/articles/10essentials.htm.

48 training cards and 24 assessment cards are disjoint; each scheduled native corpus rotates 24 training examples and adds four retention/four transfer examples without replacing previous market and general curricula. Actual family corpus sizes 120/28/28, validated with native_lora_train.validate_corpus. Source and explanation fields retained; current answer-only optimizer does not optimize explanations. Fixed assessments overlap some native holdout cards, not training examples, so repeated adaptive evaluation is not an independent external benchmark. 7B comparisons still require separate rigorous tests. Current cards are a starter bank, not exhaustive domain knowledge.

mcp/omni_knowledge.mjs defines card bank, grading, replay, augmentation; mcp/omni_worker.mjs executes one admitted structured four-question batch and prevents repeat fixed assessments per model digest. scripts/omni_cycle.mjs integrates audit/cycle into INFINITY after market curriculum and before native training. Live mode only when initial queue and bootstrap gates permit, one model per cycle. Private status/receipts under omni-academy/. No actual model-weight training or live OMNI model answers recorded yet. Existing schedules preserved.
