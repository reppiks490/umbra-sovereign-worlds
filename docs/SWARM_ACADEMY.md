# MEGASWARM — progressive academy and 7B reference (2026-11-25)

## Delivered
The owner changed the immediate target from 27B to matching or exceeding the installed Qwen2.5-Coder 7B reference on independently graded tasks. General reasoning equivalence remains unverified. A 7B model is a comparator, not a parameter target or guarantee; the 27B acquisition is unchanged.

Ten curriculum tiers across eight executable domains (80 domain-tier combinations). Each generated battery contains 16 questions. Lower tiers use progressively larger operands, reverse-ordered implication chains, precedence constraints, conditional loops, record aggregation, structured output and insufficient-information detection. Tiers 6–10 add modular arithmetic, conjunctive reasoning, scheduling with durations, weighted treatment effects, stateful modular recurrences, filtered aggregation, multi-operation formatting, and solvable versus underdetermined questions. This is a synthetic curriculum, not general-purpose intelligence certification.

mcp/swarm_difficulty.mjs defines versioned seed bands, preserving older receipts. mcp/swarm_verifier.mjs independently solves generated questions from prompt and options, never the answer key or arbitrary execution. A 1,000-seed sweep across all ten tiers verified 16,000 generated questions, and the diversity sweep confirms at least 32 distinct prompts per domain-tier. Worker/teacher receipts require agreement between generator and independent parser.

## Student-to-teacher evolution
mcp/swarm_lineage.mjs: focused apprenticeships, guided versus unassisted cycles, repeated assessment, and promotion from replayable evidence. Advancement requires 24 current-tier unassisted assessments, at least 22 fully successful, and >=32 distinct prompts from a single model digest. Specialist teacher qualification also requires >=90% unique-prompt accuracy and at most two high-confidence mistakes in that window. Teacher status is limited to its measured domain and mastered level, not general reasoning parity. Qualified mentors can generate independently verified examples for students of the other model family. Each first qualified parent may spawn one named cross-family apprentice plus a cohort expansion of 128 instances per family; parent proof receipts and generation are preserved. Active cap 4,992 per family (9,984 total), starting from 2,048 per family. No duplicate parent births. This is bounded logical population growth, not thousands of copied or independently fine-tuned base models.

mcp/swarm_reference.mjs: fixed comparator qwen2.5-coder:7b. Unassisted student prompts are replayed against exactly that model under RAM admission; missing comparator never silently substitutes a smaller model. Reference receipts remain in a separate bank, not supplied as lessons. Comparison requires 24 same-tier paired assessments, >=32 distinct prompts and stable model digests. Nonnegative mean paired accuracy delta is reported only as matched_on_specialist_probes, not statistical or general intelligence equivalence. Report groups agents without revalidating the entire corpus per agent. No real 7B comparison has executed yet.

## Adaptive parameter budget
scripts/native_lora_train.py: retention/transfer-gated adapter training now supports rank 4 -> 8 -> 16 and optimizer budget 4 -> 8 -> 16 steps only following an accepted optimization. New adapter matrices preserve prior A/B blocks, zero new B columns, and preserve alpha/r scaling. The tensor state is checked after loading; originals stay untouched; rejected candidates cannot replace accepted adapters. The real PEFT/PyTorch rank-expansion and optimizer path is pending dependency acquisition and RAM admission. Unit tests verify rank-shape and budget logic, not actual model weight training. This is family-level LoRA, not yet separate trained adapters per agent.

mcp/swarm_corpus.mjs: independently verified teacher and symbolic examples at the highest current student tier per family, cumulative replay and disjoint prompt hashes across training/retention/transfer. Separate seed bands for generated training and evaluation. Template overlap and repeated holdout adaptation remain limitations; independent external datasets and actual trained-adapter inference are still necessary.
