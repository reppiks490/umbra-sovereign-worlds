      07 / EPISTEMIC INTEGRITY

      Imagination, with evidence.

      UMBRA is a designed model, not a claim to simulate real people or predict civilizations. Its rules are explicit, its random draws are reproducible, and alternative histories use shared external shocks. Scientific credibility requires independent calibration, empirical validation, sensitivity analysis, and documented limitations.
